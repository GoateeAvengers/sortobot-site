Level progress, stars, gears, coins, styles, boosters, album cards, streaks, settings, and lives with their timestamps – one file in the app's private storage. It never leaves the device unless you enable cloud backup (below). Deleting the app deletes the file.

The app requests no permissions (no location, contacts, camera or notifications). And since we hold no data about you, there is nothing we process that could be accessed, corrected or deleted on request; your rights under the GDPR of course apply, and you can reach us at the address above.
